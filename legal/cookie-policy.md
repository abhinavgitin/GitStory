## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They are used to remember preferences and enable certain website functionality.

## 2. Cookies We Use

GitStory uses only essential, functional cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gitstory-cookie-consent` | Records that you have acknowledged the cookie notice | Essential | Persistent (localStorage) |

## 3. Cookies We Do Not Use

GitStory does not use:

- **Tracking cookies**: We do not track your browsing behavior
- **Third-party cookies**: No external services set cookies through our site
- **Advertising cookies**: We do not serve or track advertisements
- **Analytics cookies**: We do not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Hotjar, or any similar service
- **Session cookies**: We do not maintain user sessions (no login required)

## 4. Third-Party Fonts

We load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may set minimal cookies or use caching headers as part of font delivery. This is limited to font file caching and does not involve user tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

## 5. How to Control Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome**: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
- **Firefox**: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari**: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- **Edge**: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Blocking essential cookies may affect the cookie consent banner behavior but will not impact core Service functionality.
